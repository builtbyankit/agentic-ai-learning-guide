# Damaged or incorrect delivery

## Report window
Contact support within 7 calendar days after delivery when an item arrives cracked, dented, incomplete, or different from the order. Include the order reference and clear photographs of the item and packaging. Keep the item and packaging until support confirms the next step.

## Review
Support checks the report against the order and may arrange replacement, return, or refund under the applicable policy. Reporting a problem does not itself approve a refund. Do not send payment card details or account passwords in a damage report.
