# Subscription billing and cancellation

## Cancel renewal
A subscriber may turn off renewal at any time before the next billing date. Cancellation stops future renewals; access remains available until the end of the already-paid billing period. The account page displays the next renewal date in UTC.

## Refunds
Subscription fees are not prorated for unused time unless the applicable local rule or a written exception says otherwise. A cancellation confirmation should be read from the billing service before telling the user that renewal has stopped.
