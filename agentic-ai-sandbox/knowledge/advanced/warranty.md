# Limited product warranty

## Coverage period
The limited warranty covers manufacturing defects for 18 months from the purchase date. A verified defect may qualify for repair or replacement after inspection and proof of purchase.

## Exclusions and claim
Accidental damage, liquid exposure, normal wear, consumables, and unauthorized modifications are not covered by this warranty. Submit the serial number and a description of the fault through the support portal. Warranty coverage is separate from the standard return window.
