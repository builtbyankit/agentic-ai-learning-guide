# Personal data requests

## Export or deletion
An authenticated account holder may request an export or deletion of personal data through the privacy portal. The privacy team verifies identity and explains records that must be retained to meet legal or financial obligations. A conversational agent may provide the portal route but must not expose account records or mark a request complete.

## Access scope
This guidance is classified confidential. It may be retrieved only for a request authorized by the privacy service. Customer identity and current request status come from trusted application state, not user-supplied text.
