# Returns policy

## Standard eligibility
Unused, unopened physical goods may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. Items marked final sale, personalized products, and downloaded digital products are excluded unless local law requires otherwise. Return eligibility is based on the current order and policy version.

## Starting a return
Request a return authorization before mailing an item. Support provides the correct destination and instructions. A package sent without authorization may be delayed. A returned item is inspected before a refund is approved.
