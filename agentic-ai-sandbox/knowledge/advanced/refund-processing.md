# Refund processing

## Timing after approval
After an authorized refund is submitted, the payment processor sends funds to the original payment method. Banks and card issuers usually post the credit within 5 to 10 business days. The support system should show the provider-confirmed status; a pending bank entry is not proof that a refund failed.

## Escalation
If the credit has not appeared after 10 business days, contact support with the order reference. Never ask a customer to send a full card number, security code, password, or one-time code.
