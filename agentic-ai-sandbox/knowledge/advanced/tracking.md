# Tracking support

## Missing scans
Carrier scans can pause while a parcel moves between facilities. If tracking has no new scan for 5 business days, contact support for a carrier inquiry. A delayed scan alone does not mean that a parcel is lost or that a replacement has been approved.

## Current status
Use the order service for the latest fulfillment events. Tracking pages can lag behind carrier systems and are not authoritative for a refund or replacement decision.
