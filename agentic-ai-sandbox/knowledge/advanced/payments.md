# Payment authorization and card safety

## Duplicate pending entries
A pending authorization may appear as a temporary hold and can disappear within 3 to 5 business days. If two transactions settle, contact support to investigate the duplicate charge. Only the payment provider's settled transaction record determines whether money moved.

## Protect payment data
Support staff will never ask for a complete card number, card security code, password, or one-time passcode. Do not include those values in chat, support tickets, or agent traces.
