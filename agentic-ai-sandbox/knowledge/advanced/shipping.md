# Shipping and delivery estimates

## Dispatch and estimates
Most in-stock orders leave the warehouse within 1 to 2 business days. The delivery date shown at checkout or in tracking is an estimate, not a guarantee. Weather, carrier capacity, customs, and address corrections can change arrival timing.

## Address changes
An address can be changed only before the warehouse begins fulfillment. After dispatch, use the carrier's supported delivery options or contact support. Do not assume a model-provided address change has been applied; confirm it in the order system.
